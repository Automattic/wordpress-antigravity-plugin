# WordPress

Build, publish, and manage your **WordPress.com** sites and **Jetpack-connected** self-hosted WordPress sites directly from Google Antigravity.

Describe what you need and WordPress builds it: a portfolio, a restaurant menu, a blog, or an online store. Already have a WordPress.com or Jetpack site? Connect it to draft and schedule posts, edit block layouts in place, review traffic statistics, moderate comments, manage plugins, and configure domains—all through natural conversation.

---

## Key Capabilities

- **Site Creation & Provisioning** — Plan and provision new WordPress.com sites, check subdomain availability, and configure site titles and taglines.
- **Content Authoring & Block Editing** — Draft, edit, and schedule posts, pages, and custom post types (including WooCommerce products and portfolio projects) using theme-aware WordPress blocks.
- **In-Place Section Editing** — Update individual top-level block sections on existing pages and posts without rewriting the entire document.
- **Site Design & Theme Context** — Inspect active theme presets (color palettes, typography, spacing tokens), allowed blocks, templates, template parts, and navigation menus.
- **Traffic & Site Administration** — Compare monthly views and visitors, review activity logs, update site settings, manage collaborator roles and invitations, and install or update plugins.
- **Domains, DNS & Plans** — Check domain availability, configure DNS records or external mail providers (Google Workspace, iCloud Mail, Office 365, Zoho Mail), and generate WordPress.com checkout links.

## Getting Started

1. **Enable MCP Access on WordPress.com**:
   Ensure MCP access is enabled in your [WordPress.com Account Settings](https://my.wordpress.com/me/preferences/mcp) for the sites you want to manage. Self-hosted WordPress sites can also connect via [Jetpack](https://jetpack.com/).
2. **Install & Authorize**:
   Install the **WordPress** plugin in Antigravity. When prompted, sign in with your WordPress.com account via OAuth to authorize access.
3. **Start Building**:
   Invoke a starter prompt or use the bundled skills (`/edit-wordpress-block-content`, `/publish-wordpress-content`, `/manage-wordpress-com-sites`).
